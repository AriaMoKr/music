*vrlbry's release notes, kept as history: music was forked from vrlbry after this release, at `90e56f5`, and has no releases of its own. Its links are vrlbry's.*

Smoother in VR, faster with the big Wikipedias, and easier to report a problem.

**Try it:** <https://ariamokr.github.io/vrlbry/>. On a Meta Quest, open the address in its browser
and choose *Enter VR*.

**Smoother on a Quest**
- Far less garbage for the browser to collect, which caused most of the remaining dropped frames:
  across the built-in test scenarios, 250 dropped frames became 179. Walking a 10,000-book room
  went from 36 to 19, switching filters from 74 to 48, and the longest pauses from up to 49 ms to
  mostly under 15 ms.

**Big Wikipedias**
- Their indexes are a quarter of the size (the full English Wikipedia's: 74 MB → 20 MB), so the
  whole English Wikipedia (127 GB) opens from the web on a Quest in 17 s instead of 24, and in 7 s
  once its index is kept in the browser. Indexes kept from before are converted, not rebuilt.

**Start afresh, and report problems**
- *Reset stored data* (in the **?** help, and *Reset…* on the catalogue stand in VR) clears what the
  site keeps in your browser: settings, reading positions, files and addresses opened, and the web
  cache, each to keep or clear; the indexes are kept unless you choose otherwise.
- *Save screenshot* beside *Copy debug info* saves the 3D view as a picture for a bug report, and
  the debug info now says more about the graphics.
- If the browser takes the 3D view's graphics memory back (the view goes black), the page says so
  until it returns.

**Smaller things**
- On a phone, messages span the screen above the search box instead of piling up over it.
- The site's own ZIM files no longer announce themselves as new libraries on every visit.

**For those who run it:** the edge proxy logs one entry per request (the file, the mirror that
answered, the continent; nothing about the visitor) and `tools/zim-proxy/stats.mjs` prints its
requests per day.

See the [README](https://github.com/AriaMoKr/vrlbry#readme) for the controls and how to run it.
